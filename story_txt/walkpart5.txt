Once you’ve arrived at the flat, you head into your old storage room.
There it is… the old computer that belonged to you and James.
You pick up the PC and carry it into the living room.
The computer feels heavier than it should.
As if it still remembers something.
You set it down on the floor and plug it in.
After connecting the dusty computer to a monitor, a mouse and a keyboard, you press the ‘On’ button.
As the computer boots up, you think of Potter...
You’ve distrusted him from the start, ever since you met him.
Perhaps because he’s a policeman… To be honest, that’s probably the reason.
But you’ve become friends with him because he knows all the pubs owners,
and often gets free drinks for himself and his friends.
